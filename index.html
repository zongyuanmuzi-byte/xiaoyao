About

关于我About Me

专注于人像摄影与数字影像创作，以稳定而独特的视觉语言，建立情绪、光线与色彩之间的关系。

Focused on portrait photography and digital image creation, building a distinct visual language through emotion, light, and color.

专注于人像摄影与数字影像创作。多年的创作实践，使我在人物情绪捕捉、画面结构与色彩表达之间逐渐形成独特而稳定的视觉语言。

我尤其擅长人像摄影与影像调色，通过对光线、色彩与细节的精细控制，让画面呈现更具情绪张力与叙事感的视觉表达。在后期创作中，我将摄影与数字艺术相融合，以细腻的色彩塑造与视觉设计，使每一幅作品都呈现独特而完整的影像气质。

I focus on portrait photography and digital image creation. Through years of creative practice, I have gradually developed a distinctive and consistent visual language that balances emotional expression, composition, and color.

My work is particularly centered on portrait photography and color grading. By carefully controlling light, color, and visual details, I aim to create images with strong emotional intensity and narrative depth. In post-production, I combine photography with digital art techniques, shaping each image through refined color aesthetics and visual design to form a unique and cohesive visual atmosphere.
